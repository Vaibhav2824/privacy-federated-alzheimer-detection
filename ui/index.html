What was actually measured

An earlier version of this study reported accuracy above 90% on a 32-subject cohort. That number came from splitting at the slice level, which let the same brain appear in both training and test data. Rebuilding the cohort to 299 subjects and splitting by subject removed the leak, and the honest accuracy is far lower. Both numbers are reported in the paper.

Differential privacy is applied at two granularities. Sample-level DP-SGD bounds the influence of one slice. Subject-level DP-FedAvg bounds the influence of an entire patient, which is the guarantee a hospital actually wants, and is much harder to buy at a useful accuracy.

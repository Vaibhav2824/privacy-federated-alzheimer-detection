# Project completion record

What was planned, what shipped, and what is deliberately left open. This
replaces the earlier working plans, which are superseded.

## Delivered

### Methodology: subject-level differential privacy

- [x] `--dp-scope head` on `run_simulation_userlevel_dp`: the backbone is frozen
      and the Gaussian mechanism applies to the 6,147-parameter classifier head
      instead of all 23.5M parameters.
- [x] BatchNorm running statistics held fixed (momentum 0) so they stay outside
      the privacy accounting; frozen parameters copied through aggregation
      untouched, since no subject updates them.
- [x] A3 experiment group: 9 head-scope cells, ε ∈ {2, 5, 10} × seeds
      {42, 123, 2024}. The full matrix is 58/58 cells.
- [x] `dp_scope` and `perturbed_params` recorded in every metrics JSON.

Two bugs were found and fixed along the way. `get_model(freeze_backbone=True)`
keeps any parameter whose name merely *contains* `conv1` trainable, which
matches every bottleneck and left 4,338,755 parameters inside the mechanism;
the head scope now freezes on an explicit `fc.` prefix. And the aggregation
loop was adding noise to frozen parameters, which is the exact failure mode the
head scope exists to avoid.

### Results and analysis

- [x] `src/aggregate.py`: tag parser plus per-condition aggregation, replacing
      the hardcoded v1 tag lists. Emits `results_summary.json`.
- [x] `src/stats_analysis.py`: paired Wilcoxon against the correct baseline per
      mechanism. Previously, runs were classified by a `dp_mode` field that the
      centralised DP script never writes, so private runs were filed as
      non-private and overwrote the baselines they were meant to be compared
      against. Zero usable centralised-DP comparisons became seven matched pairs.
- [x] `src/figures.py`: privacy-utility curves, full-against-head scope
      comparison, convergence plots. Colour-blind-safe, chance band on every
      accuracy axis, 300 DPI.
- [x] `results_summary.json` consumed by both the paper and the web dashboard.

## Deliberately not done

- **No ADNI-derived sample slices are bundled with the demo.** The plan called
  for one-click sample images, but the only real slices available are derived
  from identifiable ADNI subjects and the data use agreement does not permit
  redistribution. The demo accepts a user-supplied image instead. Shipping a
  synthetic "MRI" was rejected as misleading.
- **The 32-subject cohort's tables remain hand-written.** Those runs predate the
  generated-table pipeline. Prose figures over that cohort are allow-listed in
  the coherence checker with their provenance rather than recomputed.

## Open questions worth pursuing

- Subject-level DP at single-digit ε needs either a far larger per-round subject
  pool or a mechanism whose noise does not scale with the parameter count.
  Low-rank or sketched updates are the obvious next thing to try.
- The expanded cohort sits only a few points above chance. Whether that is the
  cohort, the 2D-slice representation, or the label noise inherent in MCI is not
  resolved here, and 3D volumetric input is the natural next comparison.
